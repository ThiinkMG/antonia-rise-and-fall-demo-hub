Prototype · Command the siege

Take Lysa

Three minutes. Get your army over or through the walls and hold the Council Hall plaza for eight seconds.

How to command

    The siege

    • Ram breaks the gate. The gatehouse pours boiling oil on anything at its foot.
    • Archers sent at a wall, tower or gatehouse pin its defenders down: they shoot less and the oil heats slowly.
    • Siege tower docks on a wall section. Any infantry, archers too, can then cross its bridge.
    • Ladders let infantry climb anywhere, but climbers are exposed to oil. Your crew builds more in the field.
    • Clear a wall's defenders to take that section, then go down into the city.
    • Lysa will sally riders at your siege engines. Spears beat riders.

    This prototype tests control and the siege loop only. Morale, masons, day and night, and bargaining come later.
