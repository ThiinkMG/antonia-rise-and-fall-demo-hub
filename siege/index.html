The Siege of Lysa

Lysa has shut its gates under Remian banners. You have two and a half minutes and one army. Break what you can, then make them pay. A city that signs terms pays you every season. A city you burn pays you once.

Lysa's strength

How to play

  • Tap a troop card, then tap the ground below the red line.
  • Spears beat cavalry, cavalry beat archers, archers beat spears. Rams break walls.
  • Everything you break raises your leverage. Tap Parley at any time to demand tribute.
